## Search-engine protections in place

To minimise public exposure of the embedded videos:

1. The page at `camera-fundamentals.html` carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,nofollow,noarchive,nosnippet">` — major search engines (Google, Bing, etc.) will not index or cache this page.
2. The repository's root `robots.txt` disallows `/term2-video/videos/` and the teacher-only pages.
3. The page is not linked from public hub pages without the educational-use callout.

These measures are not encryption — they rely on well-behaved crawlers respecting the standard. A determined user could still find the files. The protections are a good-faith signal of restricted intent.
