DSLR Cameras Explained — From Mirror to Pro

75 minutes · Year 10 Industrial Technology — Multimedia · Term 2

IND5-2 IND5-3 IND5-9

Yesterday you learnt how cameras turn light into pictures. Today we look at the special tool you'll use in future lessons: the DSLR. What does the “SLR” mean? What's the difference between a $300 camera and a $3000 one? On Friday you'll get to hold the school's DSLR and try the mode dial yourselves — today is theory and prep.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain what makes a DSLR a “Single Lens Reflex” camera.
  • I can describe what happens inside the camera when I press the shutter.
  • I can name the three DSLR tiers and what each is best for.

Success criteria

  • I can label the mirror system in a DSLR diagram.
  • I can pick a sensible DSLR tier for a given filming job.
  • I can name what each mode-dial setting is for, ready for Friday's hands-on session.

📐 The DSLR light path

When you look through the viewfinder of a DSLR:

🌅 Light enters lens
→
🪞 Hits mirror
→
↑ Bounces up
→
👁️ Reaches your eye

When you press the shutter, the mirror flips up so light reaches the sensor instead.
